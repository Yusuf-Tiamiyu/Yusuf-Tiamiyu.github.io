About Me

A puzzle-solver
with data.

Hi, I'm Yusuf. A data analyst based in Lagos, Nigeria. I got into this field because I genuinely enjoy figuring things out. A pile of numbers is just a puzzle with the picture on the box missing.

Most of my work has been in healthcare analytics. I built a live TAT monitoring system currently in active use at a diagnostic lab network, giving Laboratory managers a real-time picture of their operations in one browser tab. I also uncovered a hidden stock crisis at a Lab, buried across 1,578 inventory records that no one had seen coming.

I strongly believe the best analysis is the kind that runs quietly in the background, so the people who need the insights can spend their time making decisions rather than assembling spreadsheets. I use Python, SQL, AI and automation to get there. Still growing in machine learning, but I figure things out and get them done.

"The goal is never just a chart. It's the decision someone makes because of it."

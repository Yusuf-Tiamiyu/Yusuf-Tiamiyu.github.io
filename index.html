About Me

Turning Data Into Decisions

I am a data analyst based in Lagos, Nigeria, with strong expertise in Excel, Power BI, Tableau, and SQL. I specialise in building dashboards and analytical tools that do not just look good — they drive real operational decisions.

My background includes healthcare analytics, where I have built live TAT (Turnaround Time) monitoring dashboards used across multiple laboratory sites. I enjoy the full pipeline — from data modelling and SQL queries all the way to Flask web apps deployed in the cloud.

Currently deepening my Python and Pandas skills and building toward machine learning. I am AI-savvy and know how to leverage the best tools to work smarter and faster.

"I don't just analyse data — I build systems that make the analysis continuous, automated, and visible to the people who need it."

  • Deployed live on Railway cloud platform

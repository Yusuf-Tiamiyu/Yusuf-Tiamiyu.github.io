About Me

Data That Actually Means Something

I'm Yusuf — data analyst, problem-solver, based in Lagos, Nigeria. I got into this because I genuinely like figuring things out. Give me a pile of numbers and I'll tell you what's actually going on inside them.

Most of my work has been in healthcare analytics. I built a live TAT monitoring dashboard that's running today across multiple lab sites at Cerba Lancet Nigeria. I built an Excel operations tracker for 9 sites. I found a stock crisis hiding in 1,578 inventory rows that nobody had spotted. The best analysis runs itself — so people focus on decisions, not spreadsheets.

I use Python and AI tools to automate the repetitive stuff. Tasks that used to take an afternoon now take minutes. Still growing (Python and ML are very much a work in progress), but I'm the type who figures it out and gets it done.

"The goal is never just a chart. It's the decision someone makes because of that chart."

Lab TAT Monitoring Dashboard

A hospital network's daily heartbeat — deployed, live, in use today.

Cerba Lancet Nigeria needed visibility across every lab site in real time. So I built it — an upload-to-dashboard pipeline where staff drop in an Excel file, it gets parsed into MySQL, and everyone from lab managers to execs can see which sites are hitting their turnaround time targets and which ones aren't. No more emailing spreadsheets back and forth.
